# Your 30-day trial: what we need from you

Please send these to your onboarding contact. Spreadsheets are fine unless a specific
format is listed. Where we give exact column names, please use them as written.

## Before day 1

| What to send | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Your registered company name, BIR TIN (up to 20 characters, e.g. 123-456-789-000) and PCAB licence number | They print on your invoices, purchase orders and BIR 2307 forms. |
| The email address of your Data Protection Officer | Required under the Data Privacy Act. We keep it on your company record. |
| A signed data processing agreement and the trial rules | You will be entering real company data. The system does not record these online yet, so we sign them on paper. |
| Up to 10 trial users: full name, work email, and the job each person does | Each person needs their own login. We give them a role: owner, admin, sales, commercial, design, project management, finance, procurement, safety, customer experience or viewer. |
| Who approves client credit checks (the finance recommendation and the president's decision), and at least two people with finance authority | Client credit checks need both decisions recorded, and some finance settings need a second person to approve them. |
| Confirmation that your mail server will accept our emails, and a test that each user receives one | Invitations, password resets and notices (quotation requests, purchase orders) arrive by email. If your mail filter blocks them, users cannot finish set-up. |
| Your usual VAT, expanded withholding tax and retention rates | Until you tell us otherwise the system uses 12% VAT, 2% withholding and 10% retention. Please confirm or correct these. |
| Your accountant's review of our starter chart of accounts and your fiscal year | Invoices, bills and stock movements post to these accounts. Accounts cannot be switched off later, so please check them first. |
| The names of your cash and bank accounts, and your main warehouse or stockroom | Payments, bank reconciliation and stock receiving use them. |
| The units you write on BOQs and purchase orders (for example "pcs", "sq.m", "lot") | We match your spellings to standard units so quantities line up across BOMs and POs. |

## Week 1

| What to send | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Your client list: company name, industry, billing address, email, phone | Every deal belongs to a client. These are entered one by one, so send the active ones first. |
| Client documents for credit checks: 3 years of audited financial statements, BIR 2303, VAT certificate, top suppliers, top clients | A deal cannot move past site survey until finance approves the client. |
| Your open deals: client, stage, contract value, gross profit, expected closing date, floor area, salesperson | So the pipeline and dashboard show your real numbers from the start. |
| The signed contract file for any deal whose project was set up before it was won | The system will not mark such a deal as won until the signed contract is on file. |
| 1 or 2 live projects: name, client, location, type (MEP, fit-out, interior or structural/civil), floor area, project code | The trial runs on real work. |
| Each project's bill of quantities as a CSV file with the columns **Description, Quantity, UOM**. Optional columns: Division, Location, Item No, Notes | This loads your quantities into the project's BOM. Please send CSV, not Excel, during the trial. Prices are added separately (next row). |
| A material list as a CSV file with the columns **code, description, unit**, and optionally **tracked** (yes/no) | It sets up your item list in one step instead of typing each item. |
| Your supplier price list: item, supplier, unit price, lead time in days, price valid from | Prices your BOMs and purchase orders. These are entered one by one, so start with the items you buy most. |
| Your supplier list: name, contact person, email, phone, address, TIN | Requests for quotation and purchase orders go to these suppliers. |
| Your standard cost codes, if you use them | We start with six: materials, labour, subcontractors, equipment, overhead and other. We add yours. |
| One month of bank statements as CSV with the columns **date, reference, description, amount** (dates as YYYY-MM-DD, money in as positive and money out as negative), plus the opening and closing balance | To test bank reconciliation. A statement whose lines do not add up to its closing balance is refused. |

## Optional

| What to send | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| A project schedule as CSV (columns: name, start_date, finish_date, predecessor_index, planned_pct_curve) or as Microsoft Project XML | Turns on progress tracking against plan. Native .mpp files are not accepted, so save as XML. |
| Contracts, permits, photos and other project files (up to 100 MB each, 500 MB per project) | Keeps the project's documents in one place. |
| CAD drawings in DXF format, drawn in metres. Keep equipment as named blocks (not exploded), and name layers by system, e.g. HVAC-EQUIP, ELEC-LIGHTING, PLUMB-FIXTURES, FIRE-SPRINKLER | The system can count equipment from drawings. This feature is limited during the trial: drawings that contain room areas may not import, so please also send the BOQ as CSV. |
| Any additional people beyond the first 10 users | We can add them during the trial if they are needed. |

**Ending the trial.** At day 30 we agree with you either to continue or to close access.
Before access is closed you can download your pipeline and finance reports (trial balance,
ledger, receivables and payables ageing) as CSV, your printed invoices and purchase orders,
and your uploaded files. Other records (projects, BOMs, purchase orders as data) can be
viewed and printed but not yet exported as files, so tell us early if you need them.
